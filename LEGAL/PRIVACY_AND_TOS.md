# Privacy Policy (Placeholder)

**Engine Alto** — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-16

## Data Collection
Engine Alto processes user-provided content (text prompts, images, code) locally on the user's machine. No data is transmitted to external servers unless the user explicitly configures cloud API keys (e.g., OpenAI).

## Local Processing
All AI inference, training, and rendering happens on the local machine or user-configured cloud GPU. No telemetry is collected.

## Third-Party Services
When `ENABLE_REAL_AI=true` and an API key is provided, prompts may be sent to third-party AI providers (OpenAI, HuggingFace). Refer to their respective privacy policies.

## Data Retention
Generated artifacts are stored locally in `data/outputs/`. Users can delete them at any time. No cloud backup is performed unless explicitly configured.

## Contact
For privacy inquiries, contact the project maintainer.
